\begin{abstract}

Redacting personally identifiable information from
identity documents is a legally mandated but technically
unsolved problem: existing cloud-based tools transmit
sensitive documents to third-party servers, text-only
systems cannot detect visual PII such as signatures
and face photographs, and no existing tool produces a
verifiable audit record of what was redacted.

We present \textbf{Omni-Shield}, a local-first
multi-modal PII redaction system that runs entirely
on consumer-grade edge hardware (NVIDIA RTX~5060,
8\,GB VRAM) without transmitting documents off-device.
The system combines a nine-agent orchestration
pipeline, a QLoRA fine-tuned vision-language model
(Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct), EasyOCR, and YOLOv8 face
detection to process six document formats --- images,
PDFs, audio recordings, plain text, Word documents,
and presentations --- with 10.42\,s mean latency for
image redaction and under 50\,ms for text and PDF.

Every redaction produces a blockchain-anchored audit
record with a salted SHA-256 commitment that prevents
document linkability, accompanied by a Groth16/BN128
zero-knowledge proof that attests to redaction
correctness without revealing the redacted values.
Blockchain verification completes in 1.2\,ms;
ZK proof generation takes 20.3\,s and executes
fully asynchronously, imposing no latency penalty
on the user-facing workflow.

We evaluate against Microsoft Presidio and Llama~3~8B
on 150 annotated synthetic identity cards and 19 real
specimen documents.
Omni-Shield achieves Macro F1\,=\,0.187 on synthetic
and 0.269 on real documents, and is the only system
to detect signatures (F1\,=\,0.870) and face
photographs (F1\,=\,0.905 on real documents) ---
categories that text-only systems cannot address
regardless of model scale.
An ablation study confirms that visual detection
agents account for 54\% of total F1, validating
the multi-modal design over text-only alternatives.
The fine-tuned adapter, evaluation harness, and
annotated test dataset are released for reproducibility.

\end{abstract}
